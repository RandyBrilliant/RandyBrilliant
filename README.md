I turn messy day-to-day operations into software people actually use. With [Trixync](https://trixync.id), I work with teams across Indonesia on inventory and production, POS, payroll, multi-branch CRMs, marketplaces, and mobile apps—often mixing web, backend, and IoT in one delivery.

When I am not shipping, I teach **database systems** to digital-business students and keep sharpening how data is modeled, stored, and queried in real products.

### What I am building now

- Growing **Trixync** as a Medan-based technology & systems studio—on-site in North Sumatra, remote-friendly across Indonesia.
- Shipping integrated ops software: stock & COGS, production workflows, POS, payroll, and reporting in single systems instead of spreadsheet chains.
- Exploring **AI inside workflows** (document extraction, routing, forecasting)—embedded in operations, not demo chatbots.
